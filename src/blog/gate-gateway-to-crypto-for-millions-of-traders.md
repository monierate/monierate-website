Gate.com, a global platform in the digital asset and financial technology space, has been listed on Monierate.com.

![Gate exchange: Gateway to Crypto For Millions of Traders](https://ik.imagekit.io/monierate/Blog/product/gate_io.webp)

This integration allows you to compare and access Gate’s rates for virtual card payments, cryptocurrency-to-fiat conversions, and digital asset trading within a single interface.

Founded in 2013 (originally as Bter.com), Gate.com has grown into a large digital asset ecosystem serving millions of users worldwide. The platform provides access to a wide range of cryptocurrencies and financial services, including virtual card solutions.

One of its key offerings is the Gate Card, a Visa-powered virtual and physical card that enables you to spend digital assets at merchants globally. This allows for payments on international platforms such as Netflix, Spotify, Amazon, AliExpress and more without requiring manual conversion beforehand.

In addition to payments, Gate offers features such as bulk gift card creation, OTC trading, and other financial tools aimed at both individual users and businesses.

As of March 26, 2026, Gate.com’s rates are shown on the Monierate platform, with a USD to Naira rate of ₦1,385.46. You are advised to review applicable fees and conversion margins, as actual transaction values may vary.

## How Can the Gate.com App Benefit You?

Gate.com offers a robust suite of tools designed for both individual users and institutional clients:

* **Gate Virtual Card:** Powered by Visa, these cards are accepted globally. Users can top up using USDT, BTC, or ETH, with real-time conversion at the point of sale. It is ideal for Facebook Ads, Google Play, international travel and a lot more.
* **Digital Asset Trading:** Access to the largest selection of altcoins in the industry (4,500+), featuring spot, futures, and margin trading.
* **Gate Pay**: A contactless, borderless, and secure mobile payment technology for merchants and users to pay and get paid in crypto.
* **Gift Card Services**: Tools for bulk gift card creation and management, allowing users to convert crypto into redeemable value for others.
* **Cold & Hot Wallet Storage:** it allows you to safeguard your assets, by using a dual-layer storage strategy that combines both speedy accessibility with institutional-grade security
* **High-Yield "Earn"**: Includes Simple Earn, Staking, and Dual Investment products to grow your idle digital assets.
* **Gate Pay for Merchants**: A no-code solution that allows businesses to accept over 300 cryptocurrencies as payment, with instant settlement in stablecoins or fiat.
* **Trading Bots**: Access to over 10 different automated strategies, including Spot Grid, Futures Grid, and Arbitrage Bots, which trade on your behalf 24/7. 
* **Gate Ventures**: A venture capital arm that invests in and supports the next generation of decentralized infrastructure and middleware. 
* **Security**: Features bank-grade encryption, 2FA, and strategy-based cold wallet storage to ensure the safety of funds.
* **24/7 Support**: Global customer service available in multiple languages via live chat and ticket systems.


## How to Get Started with Gate.com

1. **Register**: Visit [Gate.com](https://www.gate.com) or download the app from the App Store or Google Play.
2. **Identity Verification**: Complete Level 2 KYC (Identity Verification) to unlock higher limits and apply for the Gate Card.
3. **Fund Your Account**: Deposit fiat via supported P2P channels or transfer digital assets into your Gate wallet.
4. **Activate Your Card**: Locate the "Card" section to apply for your virtual Visa card for instant online spending.
5. **Explore the product:** Start trading, set up automated bots, or use "Gate Pay" for everyday transactions.
