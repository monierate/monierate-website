**Receiving international payments in Nigeria** can be slow and expensive. Stablecoins offer a faster and cheaper alternative compared to ACH and wire transfers.

This guide shows platforms you can use to receive USDT and USDC payments in Nigeria.

Many freelancers, remote workers, and digital professionals now prefer stablecoins. They are faster, global, cheaper, and easier to access.

Below are the top five platforms you can consider.

1. Vban - Free dollar, euro, & pound accounts + virtual dollar card + fast stablecoin

2. Monica - Crypto payments enabled

3. Karsa - Free virtual dollar account and card

4. Cleva - Free dollar account and virtual card

5. Breet - Very fast, stable to naira payment

## 1. Vban

**Vban** is a multi-currency platform that allows users to [**receive stablecoin payments**](https://blog.vban.com/2026/04/21/how-to-receive-crypto-payments-in-nigeria/) directly. It supports both USDT and USDC deposits across major blockchain networks.

Beyond stablecoin deposits, Vban also offers **USD, GBP, and EUR accounts**, allowing users to receive international bank transfers, e.g., from PayPal, Payoneer, Upwork, Deel, etc. It also provides a [**virtual dollar card for global payments**](https://blog.vban.com/2026/03/18/how-to-receive-dollar-payments-in-africa-2/), making it easier to manage both incoming and outgoing transactions in one place.

Users can generate a deposit address inside the app and receive payments globally. Once received, funds are automatically reflected as a USD balance, removing the need for trading or manual conversion.

This makes it easier to manage payments without using multiple apps.


#### Key Features

- Receive USDT and USDC globally

- Supports multiple networks like **TRC20, ERC20, Polygon, Solana, BEP20**

- Automatic conversion to USD balance

- Convert and withdraw to NGN, GHS, XOF, XAF

- No need for manual crypto trading

- Faster settlement compared to bank transfers

## 5. Breet

Breet is a crypto payment platform that allows users to convert digital assets into local currency. It is commonly used in Nigeria for receiving and converting stablecoin payments.

The platform focuses on making crypto-to-naira transactions simple, especially for users receiving payments from abroad.


#### Key Features

- Receive USDT and USDC payments

- Supports TRC20, ERC20, and BEP20 networks

- Instant conversion of crypto to naira

- Direct withdrawal to Nigerian bank accounts

- Digital wallet for managing crypto funds

- Designed for cross-border payments
