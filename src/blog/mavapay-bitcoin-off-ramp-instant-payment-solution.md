## **How to Get Started with Mavapay**

Getting started with Mavapay is quick and straightforward:

1. **Register:** Sign up on [www.mavapay.co](https://www.mavapay.co) or download the Mavapay app from Google Play or the App Store.

2. **Set Up Your Account:** Provide basic details like email and phone to create your wallet for holding Bitcoin, USD, or local fiat.

3. **Complete KYC:** Verify your identity with a quick ID upload for full access and higher limits; basic conversions may skip this.

4. **Deposit Assets:** Fund your wallet by sending Bitcoin via Lightning Network or adding fiat via bank transfer/mobile money.

5. **Convert or Transfer:** Select "Cash Out" to convert Bitcoin to your bank/mobile wallet, or use "Send" to transfer to recipients in supported countries. For APIs, grab your keys from the developer dashboard and integrate for automated payouts.

6. **Track & Receive:** Monitor transactions in real-time—funds hit accounts instantly, with notifications and support if needed.
