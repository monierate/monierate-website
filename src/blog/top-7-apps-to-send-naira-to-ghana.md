Sending money from Nigeria to Ghana has become significantly easier in 2026. You no longer need to find a "mallam" or convert your Naira to Dollars first. 

In this article, I break down the seven best apps that allow you to send Naira (NGN) directly, and your recipient receives Ghana Cedis (GHS) in their bank account or Mobile Money (MoMo) wallet.

1. Momo (by MTN)
2. Hizo Africa
3. Bitnob 
4. Cashwyre 
5. Eversend
6. Send App (by Flutterwave)

### **1. Momo (by MTN)**

|                  |                              |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------- |
| ⭐ App Rating     | 4.3 (App Store & Play Store) |
| 💱 Exchange Rate | ₦128.205 / GHS               |
| ⚡ Transfer Speed | Under 10 minutes             |

MTN MoMo is a versatile financial platform that empowers you to send and receive funds, settle bills, and receive international remittances. Beyond payments, it provides seamless access to microloans and high-interest savings directly from your mobile device.

**Pros**

- Instant Settlement

- No Internet Required as you can dial the USSD code (\*671#)

- Offers Automatic Conversion

- Competitive fees with MoMo’s cross-border fees generally hovering around 3% to 4%.

**Cons**

- You cannot send or receive international funds on a "Tier 1" (basic) account. 
- It is not ideal for bulk transactions,  as you cannot send more than the equivalent of **$2,000–$5,000** per month (depending on your tier)
- While the "fee" is low (3%), the exchange rate offered is usually slightly higher than the black market rate.

### **Which App Should You Use?**

Most of these apps will get your Naira to Ghana, but the best one depends on your specific situation.

**For the best exchange rate,** go with **Bitnob**. As it offers the most value for your money, and the 1% flat fee is incredibly transparent for smaller transfers.

**For the fastest settlement,** **Cashwyre** is the leader. It consistently hits the "under 5 minutes" mark, which is vital if you're sending money for an urgent bill or emergency.

**For the most reliable reach,** **MTN MoMo** is your best bet. Because it uses the USSD infrastructure (\*671#), it works even when the internet is down, making it the most accessible option for recipients in rural areas.

**For large, formal transfers,** use **Send App (by Flutterwave)**. It offers the high reliability of the Flutterwave network and is perfect for bigger business transactions.

**For regular family support,** **Chipper Cash** and **Eversend** are excellent. Chipper offers free instant transfers if both people use the app, while Eversend’s multi-currency wallet allows you to "buy" Cedis when the rate is good and hold them until you're ready to send.

**Bottom line:** If you want the perfect balance of price and speed, start with **Bitnob**. If you are offline or sending to a non-smartphone user, go with **MTN MoMo**.
