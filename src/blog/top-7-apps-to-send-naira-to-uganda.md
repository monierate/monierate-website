If you need to send money from Nigeria to Uganda, the key question is, how do you transfer your naira and get it delivered quickly?

The Naira is not used in Uganda, and traditional bank transfers are often slow, expensive, and unpredictable.

The good news is you can now send naira and have it received in Ugandan Shillings (UGX) within minutes using the right apps, no need for cash exchanges.

To save you time, here are 7 of the best apps to send money from Nigeria to Uganda, fast, reliable, and without the usual stress.


## My top 7 Apps to pay anyone in Uganda with naira

1. Bitnob
2. Accrue
3. Eversend
4. Flutterwave Send
5. Momo (By MTN)
6. Hizo Africa
7. Chipper Cash


### 1. Bitnob

|                  |                               |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------- |
| ⭐ App Rating     |  3.9 (App Store & Play Store) |
| 💱 Exchange Rate | ₦0.38 / UGX                   |
| ⚡ Transfer Speed | Under 20 minutes              |

Bitnob is an app that uses Bitcoin and the Lightning Network to move money fast and cheaply across Africa.

**Pros**

- Send NGN to Uganda bank accounts
- Offers near-instant settlement
- Supports more African countries for cross-border payments

**Cons**

- Some users report that customer support can be slow to resolve issues
- Verification issues are occasionally reported by users
- 1% fee for all intra-Africa transfers

### 5. Momo (by MTN)

|                  |                              |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------- |
| ⭐ App Rating     | 4.3 (App Store & Play Store) |
| 💱 Exchange Rate | ₦0.39/ UGX                   |
| ⚡ Transfer Speed | Under 15 minutes             |

MTN MoMo is a financial platform that empowers you to send and receive funds, settle bills, and receive international remittances across Africa. 

**Pros**

- Instant Settlement
- No Internet Required, as you can dial the USSD code (\*671#)
- Offers Automatic Conversion

**Cons**

- You cannot send or receive international funds on a "Tier 1" (basic) account. 
- It is not ideal for bulk transactions,  as you cannot send more than the equivalent of **$2,000–$5,000** per month (depending on your tier)
- While the "fee" is low (3%), the exchange rate offered is usually slightly higher than the black market rate.

### Which App Should You Use?

Most of these apps will get your Naira to Uganda, but the best one depends on your specific situation.

For the best exchange rate, go with Send App (by Flutterwave) or Chipper Cash. They currently offer the highest amount of UGX per Naira. 

Bitnob is the top choice if you want near-instant, borderless transfers with a flat fee.

For the fastest settlement, Accrue is the leader, as it consistently delivers using a specialised agent network to ensure payouts hit Ugandan wallets in under 5 minutes.

For the most reliable reach, MTN MoMo is your best bet. 

For a global, professional experience, use Send App (by Flutterwave). 

For regular support and savings, Eversend is excellent. Their multi-currency wallet allows you to hold both NGN and UGX simultaneously.

Happy sending!
