Afriex is a fast remittance platform focused on the African diaspora. It is designed for you to be able to send money across borders instantly without the high costs associated with traditional banks.  

**Pros**

- Transfers arrive in less than 20 seconds

### **Which App Should You Use?**

Most of these apps will get your Naira to the UK, but the best one depends on your specific situation.

For the best exchange rate, go with Pesa and Bitnob. Pesa focused on a "zero-fee" model and they both provide rates that are consistently the closest to the free market average.

For the fastest settlement, Afriex is the leader. It is built for extreme speed, with 99% of transfers arriving in under 20 seconds.

For large, formal transfers, use Send App (by Flutterwave). It offers the high reliability of the Flutterwave network and provides larger transaction limits for verified users.
