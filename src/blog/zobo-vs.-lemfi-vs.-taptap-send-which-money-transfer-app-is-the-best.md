We tested all three apps by sending $200 from the US to Lagos. Here’s what we found.

Many people compare apps like LemFi, Taptap Send, and Zobo to find the right option for their needs.

## **Zobo**

**Zobo** is built for the African diaspora sending money from the US, UK, and Europe. It supports transfers to countries like Nigeria and Francophone regions using XOF and XAF.

Users abroad can send funds in **USD, GBP, or EUR**, while recipients in Africa receive the money in their **local currency,** such as NGN, XOF, or XAF, directly into their bank account or mobile wallet. The platform shows the full payout amount before confirming the transaction.

Zobo focuses on transparency and simplicity. It also offers virtual wallets for EUR and GBP users.

#### Key features:

- Zero transaction fees

- Rate shown before confirmation

- USD, GBP, and EUR supported

- NGN, XOF, XAF delivery

- Real-time transfer tracking

- Fund transfers via debit/credit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or bank transfer

- Send money to local bank accounts or mobile money wallets

- Fast and secure transfers

- Competitive FX rates

Strong for: Users who want clear rates and Africa-focused transfers.


## **Taptap Send**

**Taptap Send** is a remittance app with global coverage. It allows users to send money from the US, UK, Canada, and Europe to over 50 countries.

The platform is known for speed and simplicity. Transfers are often completed within minutes on supported routes.

It supports multiple currencies and payment options. This makes it useful for users sending to different regions.

#### Key features:

- 50+ destination countries

- USD, GBP, EUR, CAD, AUD, AED supported

- Fast transfers, often within minutes

- Bank transfer, debit card, and wallet options

- Simple mobile interface

Strong for: Users who need broad global coverage.


## **LemFi**

**LemFi** is a digital transfer platform available in the US, UK, and Canada. It supports transfers to African countries and some global destinations.

The platform offers a digital-first experience. It also includes account-style features alongside transfers.

LemFi advertises zero fees. However, exchange rate differences can affect the final payout depending on the corridor.

#### Key features:

- Supports transfers across multiple regions

- USD, GBP, EUR, CAD supported

- Bank transfer and debit card funding

- Digital account-style features

- Transfers to African and global destinations

Strong for: Users who want a digital banking-style experience.


## **Which App Is Cheaper for Sending Money to Africa?**

The cost of sending money depends on both fees and exchange rates. Some platforms claim zero fees but adjust their FX rates behind the scenes.

**Zobo** offers **zero transaction fees**, which removes extra charges that reduce the final payout. It also provides **competitive FX rates**, meaning your recipient receives more money compared to platforms with hidden markups.

By combining no fees with transparent and strong exchange rates, Zobo ensures more value reaches the recipient, making it [**the cheapest way to send money to Africa**](https://blog.zobo.money/2026/04/21/how-to-save-money-on-remittance-when-sending-money-to-africa/).


## Which App Is Faster for Sending Money to Africa?

Transfer speed depends on how the payment is funded. Card payments are usually the fastest and can arrive within minutes, while bank transfers may take longer depending on the corridor.

**Zobo** supports fast transfers by allowing users to fund payments through debit or credit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or bank transfer. Card-funded transactions are processed quickly, and in many cases, funds reach the recipient in minutes.

With efficient processing and direct payouts to local bank accounts or mobile wallets, Zobo ensures recipients receive funds as quickly as possible.

If you need an alternative to LemFi and Taptap Send, Zobo is a suitable option for sending money to Africa.

It also supports multiple payment methods and real-time tracking, which makes it suitable for users who send money regularly.
