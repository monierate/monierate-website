Are you looking for the best app to receive stablecoins in Nigeria? 

Stablecoins are digital currencies (like USDT, USDC, or cNGN) designed to stay worth roughly $1 or ₦1 each, so unlike Bitcoin, which can jump or crash wildly, your salary, loans, or payment in stablecoins should feel almost the same as receiving dollars or naira without big unexpected changes in value.

To save you time and money, I've carefully selected the seven best apps you can use to receive stablecoins in Nigeria. These apps are ranked based on speed, security, fees, and ease of use, using real-world testing.

By the end of this guide, you’ll know exactly which app to use to secure your funds instantly.


## Top 7 Apps to Receive Stablecoins in Nigeria (2026)

1. **Karsa** - Free virtual dollar account and card

2. **Breet** - Very fast, stable to naira payment

3. **Spenda**  - Spend your stablecoins like cash

4. **PalRemit** - Support multiple networks

5. **nsave** - Invest in stocks with stablecoins

6. **Spendin** - Convert to South African Rand or Naira

7. **Cash-in** - Instant conversion to your bank account


### **1. Karsa -** 4.1 (Play Store)

Karsa is designed to receive and earn interest in stablecoins such as USDT and USDC. It supports the most commonly used networks by Nigerians. The app offers a clean experience for receiving, holding, and converting stablecoin to Naira.

Unlike apps that force an immediate swap to Naira, [Karsa](https://monierate.com/exchanges/karsa) provides a high-yield USD account, allowing users to hold digital dollars while earning 4-5% APY.

**Pros**

- Easy conversion to Naira

- Free virtual dollar account and card

- Earn interest in dollars

**Cons**

- Not many advanced trading features

- Some crypto networks may not always be available


### **2. Breet -** 4.5 (App store and Play store)

[Breet](https://monierate.com/exchanges/breet) is best if your main goal is to receive stablecoins and cash out immediately.\
Once your USDT is confirmed, your naira is paid almost instantly.

You don’t need to wait for buyers or negotiate prices.

**Pros**

- Very fast naira payment

- Easy to use

- Reliable for USDT

**Cons**

- Not good for holding funds long-term

- Supports fewer stablecoins


### **3. Spenda -** 4.6 (App store and Play store)

[Spenda](https://monierate.com/exchanges/spenda) makes spending crypto feel like cash; it is effortless. You convert cryptocurrency to cash in real-time for instant bank transfers and bill purchases.

**Pros**

- Spend your stablecoins like cash

- Pay your bills and send bank transfers quickly

- Supports multiple networks

**Cons**

- Conversion fees apply

- Not many complex tools are available

- Relatively new with a growing customer base


### **4. PalRemit** - 3.8 (App store and Play store)

If you care about major network support, then open a [PalRemit](https://monierate.com/exchanges/palremit) account. You can receive USDT and USDC via different crypto networks, such as TRC20, ERC20, BEP20, MATIC, and more.

This makes it easier when the sender can only use a less popular network.

**Pros**

- Supports many networks

- Flexible deposit options

- Transactions are reliable

**Cons**

- Fees depend on the network used

- App design is basic


### **5. nsave** - 4.3 (App store and Play store)

[nsave](https://monierate.com/exchanges/nsave) is best if you want to keep your stablecoins and earn from them. Instead of rushing to convert to naira, you can hold your USDT or USDC (USD-backed) and let it grow over time.

**Pros**

- Allows you earn on stablecoins

- Get a free US Dollar account

- Invest in stocks

**Cons**

- Slower access to the naira

- Not good for frequent withdrawals


### **6. Spendin** - 4.7 (App store and Play store)

Spendin is a wallet-focused app that lets you receive stablecoins, send them to your naira account, and even send them across Nigeria.

It’s built for everyday use, not complicated trading.

**Pros**

- Simple to receive USDT and USDC and spend

- Send to Naira and South African Rand accounts

- Good for daily transactions

**Cons**

- Conversion features are still limited

- Not many advanced tools


### **7. Cash-In** - 4.6 (App store and Play store)

[Cash-In](https://monierate.com/exchanges/cash_in) is built for quick crypto-to-naira conversion, allowing you to send stablecoins like USDT or USDC. Your Naira is paid directly to your bank account.

The app does not work like a wallet; it focuses on fast deposits and instant payout, making it useful for simple cash-out needs.


### **Pros**

- Fast conversion to Naira

- 0% Service fee

- Direct bank payout

- Simple and easy to use, with low KYC requirements


### **Cons**

- Not a storage wallet

- Limited holding options
