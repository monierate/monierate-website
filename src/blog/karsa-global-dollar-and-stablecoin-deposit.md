Karsa, a growing platform in the global neobanking sector, has been officially listed on [**monierate.com**](http://monierate.com).

![Global Dollar Account and Stablecoin Deposit](https://ik.imagekit.io/monierate/Blog/product/karsa.webp)

This listing allows you to compare Karsa’s competitive dollar yields and virtual card rates alongside other top-tier fintechs, providing a one-stop shop for freelancers and remote workers looking to hedge against inflation and manage global payments.

Founded in 2024 and backed by**Y Combinator**, Karsa was built to close the gap between emerging markets and global financial infrastructure. Amongst its offering is a  "Global Dollar Account" that allows you to move away from local banking systems and embrace a more stable, digital-first economy.

Karsa’s standout feature is its **integrated USD banking**, which provides you with a virtual U.S. bank account to receive international payments directly from platforms like Upwork, Fiverr, or global employers. 

Combined with their **Virtual Visa Card**, you can spend your earnings anywhere globally, from online subscriptions to international travel, with little to no friction and zero hidden fees.

With a growing user base across Nigeria, Pakistan, and Kenya, and a focus on transparency and yield, Karsa is quickly becoming a favorite for those who value speed, security, and the ability to earn on their dollar savings.

## **How Can the Karsa App Benefit You?**

Karsa is specifically designed to handle the complexities of cross-border finance for you as an African professional:

- **Free Virtual U.S. Bank Account:** For free you get to create a US bank account where you can receive your salary or freelance payments in USD just like a local. No need for complex domiciliary accounts.

- **Free Virtual Visa Card:** Get a high-acceptance card for global spending. Whether it's paying for **Adobe , Netflix, Facebook Ads and more**, Karsa’s card is optimized for minimal declines.

- **High-Yield Savings:** Protect your income from Naira volatility by holding it in digital dollars while earning a competitive **4–5% annual yield**.

- **Fast Crypto-to-Fiat Ramps:** Seamlessly convert between stablecoins (like USDT/USDC) and local currency for quick withdrawals to your Nigerian bank account.

- **Zero Hidden Fees:** Karsa prides itself on a transparent fee structure, particularly for card creation and maintenance, making it a cost-effective choice for regular spenders.

- **Instant Verification:** A streamlined onboarding process that allows users to set up their global account in minutes using standard identification.


## **How to Get Started with Karsa**

1. **Download & Sign Up:** Get the Karsa app from the Google Play Store or iOS App Store.

2. **KYC Verification:** Complete the simple identity check to unlock your Virtual U.S. Bank Account details.

3. **Fund Your Account**: You can fund your wallet via crypto (USDT/USDC) or receive direct USD transfers from abroad.

4. **Issue Your Card**: Locate the "Cards" tab to generate your virtual Visa card instantly.

5. **Spend or Save**: Use your card for online payments or leave your balance in the account to start earning daily interest.
