Are you a Nigerian travelling to Rwanda, or are you simply looking to make transactions from Nigeria?

If you’ve ever tried sending naira to Rwanda, you’ll know the typical process: walking into a bank, converting naira to dollars, submitting documentation, paying high fees, and then waiting days for the money to arrive.

In this article, I break down the 5 best apps for sending naira to Rwanda, fast, easy to use, and without the heavy charges that come with banks.

## My Top 5 Apps to Send Naira to Rwanda

- Momo (By MTN)
- Hizo Africa
- Bitnob
- Cashwyre
- Eversend

**Ranking**: The ranking below is based on overall user app stores rating, exchange rate fees, and transfer speed.

MTN MoMo is a versatile financial platform that empowers you to send and receive funds, settle bills, and receive international remittances across Africa.

## Which App Should You Use?

Most of these apps will get your Naira to Rwanda, but the best one depends on your specific situation.

For the best exchange rate, go with Bitnob. They offer the most value for your money, and their transparent fee structures are best for smaller, frequent transfers.

For the most reliable reach, MTN MoMo is your best bet. Because it uses USSD infrastructure (\*671#), it works even when the internet is spotty, making it ideal for recipients in rural Rwanda.

For a global, professional experience, use Paysend. If you are sending to a bank card (Visa/Mastercard) rather than a wallet, Paysend offers the highest reliability.

For regular support and savings, Eversend is excellent. Their multi-currency wallet allows you to "buy" RWF when the rate is good and hold them until you’re ready to spend.
