## **Which App Should You Use?**

Most of these apps will get your Naira to Kenya — but the best one depends on your specific situation.

**For the best exchange rate**, go with **Mavapay** or **Hizo Africa**. Both offer ₦10.92/KSh, the most competitive rate in this list, and both settle in under 10 minutes. Mavapay edges ahead slightly on speed, making it the top pick overall.

**Eversend** and **Bitnob** are your most reliable options. Their infrastructure is purpose-built for mobile money, and both have a proven track record with Kenyan recipients. Bitnob also keeps fees low at a flat 1% for all Africa-to-Africa transfers.

**For simplicity**, **Hizo Africa** is the friendliest option for first-time senders. Its clean interface walks you through everything without needing any crypto knowledge — ideal if you just want to support family back home.

**For bills, utilities, and everyday spending**, **Cashwyre** and **Mavapay** are built for exactly this use case. Fast settlement, low fees, and no unnecessary currency conversion steps make them efficient for regular, smaller transfers.

**For frequent cross-border travel across multiple African countries**, **Spendin** or **Eversend** give you the most flexibility. Both support multi-currency wallets, so you can hold and send across several currencies without setting up a new account each time.

**Spendin offer broad asset funding options (Naira, USDT, USDC). But it rate** — at ₦13.05/KSh, is noticeably less competitive than the rest of the pack.

**Bottom line**: If you want one recommendation, start with **Mavapay** — best rate, fastest speed, and lowest fees.
