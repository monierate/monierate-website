Sending money to Africa from the US can be expensive and confusing. This guide shows how to choose the right platform based on fees, speed, and ease of use.

Many people send money regularly to countries like Nigeria, Ghana, and Cameroon. The main concern is usually how to get the best value without losing money to fees or poor exchange rates.

Several apps now offer cross-border transfers to Africa. Below are three commonly used options and how they compare.


### 1. Zobo

[Zobo](https://monierate.com/exchanges/zobo_money) is a money transfer app used by diaspora users in the US, UK, and Europe. It supports transfers to African countries, including Nigeria, Ghana, and Cameroon.

Users can send money in USD, GBP, or EUR and receive funds in local currencies such as NGN, XOF, and XAF. The platform is designed to provide a simple and clear transfer process.

**Key features of Zobo:**

- Zero transaction fees

- Real-time exchange rate display before confirmation

- Support for USD, GBP, and EUR transfers

- Bank account and mobile money delivery options

- Multiple payment methods, including debit cards and ACH

- Virtual EUR and GBP wallets for European users

- Real-time transfer tracking


## 2. Sendwave

Sendwave is a remittance app that allows users to send money from the US, Canada, the UK, and the EU to over 50 countries worldwide, including several African destinations.

It is known for fast transfer speeds and a simple mobile experience. Many users rely on it for quick and frequent transfers.

**Key features of Sendwave:**

- Fast transfer speeds, often within minutes

- Simple and easy-to-use mobile app

- Available in the US, Canada, UK, and EU

- Supports transfers to over 50 countries globally


## 3. LemFi

LemFi is a digital money transfer platform available in the US, UK, Canada, and parts of Europe. It supports international transfers to, across, and beyond African countries.

Users can send money to destinations such as Nigeria and Ghana, as well as other supported regions. The platform focuses on providing a straightforward digital experience.

**Key features of LemFi:**

- Supports international transfers across multiple regions

- Available in the US, UK, Canada, and Europe

- Bank account funding options

- Digital-first user experience

- Transfers to African and other global destinations

## What Should You Consider Before Choosing?

When choosing a platform, focus on the total value of your transfer. Fees and exchange rates both affect how much your recipient receives.

Some platforms offer zero fees but adjust exchange rates. Others prioritize speed but may limit payment options. It is important to compare both cost and convenience. If you send money regularly, even small differences in fees or rates can add up over time.

You can choose based on what matters most to you. All three platforms support transfers to Africa.

Use Zobo if you want zero fees and clear rates. Use Sendwave if speed is your main priority. Use LemFi if you prefer a simple digital setup.
